# ShareYourMons Roadmap

ShareYourMons started as a way to see an entire Cobblemon PC collection without opening every box.

The goal is to keep making it easier to find, organize, and share the Pokémon that matter to you.

This roadmap shows what I’m exploring and where I want to take the project. It is not a release schedule, and features may change as development progresses.

## Recently Completed

### Collection Viewer

* Browse your PC collection in Minecraft.
* Find custom skins, Legendary and Mythical Pokémon, and shinies.
* See optional PC box and slot locations.
* Copy a collection report to your clipboard.
* Refresh the view without restarting Minecraft.

### Classification Research

* Built a reviewed catalog of Cobblemon Town skin collections.
* Investigated differences between community names and game identifiers.
* Improved handling of unknown or conflicting appearance information.
* Added automated checks for classification and report behavior.

## Proposed Features Under Consideration

These are exploratory ideas, not promised features or release dates.

### Advanced IV Filtering

Find 5x31 and 6x31 Pokémon more easily.

### Ability Display and Hidden Ability Identification

Show ability information in the collection view and make Hidden Abilities easier to identify.

### Alpha Pokémon Identification

Mark Alpha Pokémon clearly in the collection view.

### Pokédex Integration

Connect collection browsing with Pokédex progress.

### Live Touch Trade Finder

Help players find touch trades for Pokédex completion.

### Better PC Browsing

Add clearer filtered list views for larger collections.

### Improved Individual and Multiple-Pokémon Sharing

Make it easier to share one Pokémon or a small group, including compact Minecraft chat sharing.

## Ongoing Improvements

### Cobblemon Town Content

Continue researching new skins, custom Pokémon, and forms as reliable information becomes available.

### Classification Accuracy

Improve the catalog while preserving the distinction between confirmed information and unresolved observations.

### Collection Viewer

Improve usability, organization, and report presentation based on testing and player feedback.

### Compatibility

Investigate compatibility with additional versions and environments when practical.
